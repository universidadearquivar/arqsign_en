<details>

<summary>What legislation regulates electronic signatures in Brazil?</summary>

Provisional measure MP 2.200-2 of 2001 is the first and main Brazilian law that regulates electronic signatures.&#x20;

This support is present in article 10 and requires compliance with 3 requirements for the legal validity of the electronic signature, as detailed below:&#x20;

1. First requirement of MP 2002-2 for the legal validity of electronic signatures: Proof of authorship of signatures. In the ArqSign platform process, all data collected about each person who signs the document is recorded in the file properties, proving the authorship of each signature.&#x20;
2. Second requirement of MP 2002-2 for the legal validity of the electronic signature: Proof of the integrity of the document. The ArqSign Platform is the only one on the market that applies a digital certificate to each person's signature even if they do not have a digital certificate. This process allows proof of the integrity of the document between signatures and at the end of the process.&#x20;

<summary>Does an electronic signature have legal validity?</summary>

Provisional Measure No. 2.200-2/2001 created the Brazilian Public Key Infrastructure (ICP-Brasil) and determined the criteria to guarantee legal validity.  &#x20;

These criteria are described in article 10, § 2 of the aforementioned MP and are: &#x20;

* Guarantee of document integrity; &#x20;
* Guarantee of authorship of the signature; &#x20;
* Accepted for electronic signature by all parties.&#x20;
