<summary>How to configure a document to be signed with a digital certificate (digital signature) or without a digital certificate (electronic signature)?</summary>

On the ArqSign Platform, when configuring a signature flow, you can determine which type of signature should be executed by each recipient by selecting one of the following options:w the steps below:

a) **Electronic Signature** (ArqSign generates advanced electronic signatures with legal validity in accordance with MP 2.200-2 of 08/24/2001 and Law 14.063 of 11/23/2020);

b) **Signature with Personal Digital Certificate ICP-Brasil Type** (ArqSign generates qualified digital signatures in accordance with MP 2.200-2 of 08/24/2001 and Law 14.063 of 11/23/2020);

c) **Signature with Personal Digital Certificate (All Types)** (ArqSign generates both electronic and digital signatures using other certificates).

**To determine the signature type, follow these steps:**

1. After uploading the document and configuring the necessary settings, proceed to configure the recipients.
2. When configuring a recipient, in the "Signature Type" field, select one of the options described above.
3. Done! Now, just configure the other recipients and the signature position on the document, then send.

To learn how to sign a document with a Digital Certificate – ICP-Brasil,[ click here](https://app.gitbook.com/o/Ai1YjbPQxIuvTaVzoZ4H/s/WoZ5sO9ix7pAjCll866S/~/changes/159/digital-certificate).

<summary>How to sign a document with a Digital Certificate - ICP-Brasil?</summary>

On the ArqSign Platform, the document sender can specify the type of signature that the recipient must execute, selecting from the following options:

a) **Electronic Signature:** ArqSign generates advanced electronic signatures, legally valid in accordance with MP 2.200-2 (08/24/2001) and Law 14.063 (11/23/2020);

b) **Personal Digital Certificate - ICP-Brasil Type:** ArqSign generates qualified digital signatures in compliance with MP 2.200-2 (08/24/2001) and Law 14.063 (11/23/2020);

c) **Personal Digital Certificate - All Types:** ArqSign generates both electronic and digital signatures using other types of certificates.

If you receive a document to sign via the ArqSign Platform and need to use a Digital Certificate for the first time, follow these steps:

* Open the document, read it and if you accept it, click Sign;
* Execute the signature in the format of your preference and click Next;
* When clicking “Next”, you will be informed that the requested signature must be executed with a digital certificate;
* Select which certificate will be used to sign the document using the options:

1\) Certificates that have been entered into ArqSign and are saved in the cloud and

2\) Certificates saved on the computer of the person signing the document.

<summary>How to add ArqSign Digital Certificate extension in Edge browser?</summary>

1. When a signatory of a document requiring a digital certificate—whether ICP-Brasil or any other—applies its visual representation and clicks the “Next” button, a message will display informing them that, to sign the document with a Digital Certificate installed on their computer, they must first add the extension to their browser and install the desktop module. The process is straightforward. Simply click the displayed link and follow the instructions:
2. In Microsoft Edge, clicking the available link may trigger pop-up blocking.
3. If this occurs, click the pop-up blocker icon next to the page URL.
4. Once clicked, allow pop-ups for the ArqSign page.
5. After unblocking pop-ups, click the available link again. This action initiates two simultaneous processes: the first is the download of the Desktop Module installer. Wait for the download to complete, then follow the steps in the Desktop Module Installation Tutorial. The second action opens a Microsoft Store tab with the ArqSign plugin.
6. Click the “Get” button, and a notification will confirm that the extension has been added to your browser.
7. Click “Add Extension,” and a notification will confirm the addition of the extension to the browser. Afterward, the Microsoft Store tab can be closed.
8. If the Desktop Module is not yet installed, the application will still display a link for its download.
9. Once both installations are complete, the modal will update, listing the certificates saved on the user's computer.
10. In Windows, when clicking the “Finish” button, you may be asked to grant permission for the plugin to access the certificates. At this point, click “Allow” to permit the use of digital certificates.

If the extension and desktop module are already installed, this action will not be required again.

If the signatory has Digital Certificates saved on the ArqSign platform, they will appear. To use them, simply select the desired certificate and proceed with the next steps, without the need to install the extension and module.

<summary>Should I use an ICP-BRASIL digital certificate whenever I sign a document electronically?</summary>

No. To sign documents between private entities, such as documents between companies and individuals, companies and companies, or clinics and patients, you can use an electronic signature, which is a virtual signature that does not require the ICP-BRASIL digital certificate.

<summary>Is a digital certificate the same thing as a digital signature?</summary>

No, they are different things, although they are interconnected.

While the digital certificate is the equivalent of the CPF or CNPJ identity document, the digital signature is the equivalent of your physical signature with notarization.

Because it is directly linked to the digital certificate, the digital signature ensures authenticity, integrity, reliability, and non-repudiation.

The digital signature may lose its validity if any changes are made to the digital document.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is a digital certificate?</summary>

A digital certificate is a virtual identity, similar to a CPF or CNPJ document, used to prove your identity electronically. It allows an electronically made signature to be unequivocally identified as yours. The document is protected by a system of two cryptographic keys, one private and one public, which are unique to each certificate. One key decrypts the other, enabling identification.

To acquire a digital certificate, you must contact a certifying agency or authority. The costs for issuing the document will be provided by the institution itself. ArqSign is not a certifying agency or authority, but rather a platform for signing documents with or without a digital certificate. executed electronically with a digital certificate is defined as a digital signature, while one executed electronically without a digital certificate is referred to as an electronic signature.

</details>

<summary>How do I import/upload my ICP-Brasil Type A1 Certificate to the platform?</summary>

1. Access your account on the ArqSign Platform.
2. Click on the Profile Menu located in the upper-right corner of the Platform.
3. Select the "Digital Certificate" tab.
4. Click the "+" button.
5. Choose the ICP-Brasil Certificate file type A1 with P12 or PFX extension.
6. Enter a name to identify the Certificate.
7. Enter the Certificate password.
8. When signing a document with an ICP-Brasil Certificate, simply select the certificate and enter your password.
