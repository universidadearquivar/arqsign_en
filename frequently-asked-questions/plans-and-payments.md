# 🟪 Plans and payments

<details>

<summary>Can I cancel my plan if I need to?</summary>

All plans can be canceled at any time.

For monthly plans, simply disable the automatic renewal on the platform itself.

For annual plans during the term, just contact us at faleconosco@arqsign.com.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the advantage of purchasing an annual plan?</summary>

The advantage of purchasing an annual plan is a 20% discount on the total amount, which can be paid in up to 12 interest-free installments.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I sign up for a plan with WhatsApp shipping?</summary>

When you sign up for an ArqSign plan, you will have access to a certain number of Submissions depending on the plan. These Submissions can be made via Email or WhatsApp under the following conditions:

To send flows via WhatsApp, the account must have the **Sending and WhatsApp Credits** available. This is because, in this case, the flow is sent through an integration with the official WhatsApp service. To purchase WhatsApp credits, go to the [Buy Credits](../top-menu/buy-credits.md) menu. The cost is R$0.45 per message sent. When sending a flow, 2 message**s** are consumed per signatory—one for sending the document for signature and one for sending the signed document at the end of the process.

To send flows via email, the account only needs the **Sending** **option** enabled, as the ArqSign platform itself handles the sending of the flow.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the billing basis for each plan?</summary>

The basis for charging each plan is the service of sending a flow for subscription.

A flow submission can contain multiple files and signatures, and still, only one submission will be deducted from the plan.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How does recurring payment for ArqSign plans work?</summary>

For monthly plans, you pay a monthly fee when you sign up for the plan and start using the tool immediately. Every month, on the same day of purchase, the monthly fee will be automatically charged to the same card, unless you disable automatic renewal.

For annual plans, there is the option to purchase in up to 12 interest-free installments on your card.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How to hire the ArqSign Platform?</summary>

It's very simple! Choose the plan that best suits your needs, click on "Start now," fill in the billing and account creation details, and complete the purchase.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I change my annual plan?</summary>

You can change your annual plan 30 days before it is due to renew. To do so, click on the “Change plan” or “Buy now” button, choose the plan you want to continue with, and make the payment as usual.

If you need to change it before, please contact faleconosco@arqsign.com.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I change my monthly plan?</summary>

Five days before your monthly plan is due to renew, you can change it. To do so, click on the "Change plan" or "Buy now" button, choose the plan you want to continue with, and make the payment as usual.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I reactivate my account after the plan expires?</summary>

To reactivate a plan whose renewal has expired, the customer must click the "Change plan" or "Buy now" button, choose the plan they want to continue with, and make the payment as usual.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens if I don't renew my plan?</summary>

After the plan expires, if it is not renewed, the customer has an additional 10 days to use the remaining credits and/or additional credits.

When the customer reactivates the plan, the credits for additional shipments within the 6-month validity period will be activated, along with the credits for the plan chosen for reactivation.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I cancel a monthly plan?</summary>

Simply go to the [Administration > Account > Billing and Usage](../administration/administration/account.md#billing-and-usage-tab) menu and disable the Auto-Renewal option.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I cancel an annual plan during its validity period?</summary>

Please contact [faleconosco@arqsign.com](mailto:faleconosco@arqsign.com) .

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I cancel the automatic renewal of a monthly or annual plan?</summary>

Simply go to the [Administration > Account > Billing](../administration/administration/account.md#billing-and-usage-tab) menu and disable the Auto-Renewal option.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there loyalty to the plans?</summary>

No. All plans can be cancelled or renewed according to the customer's needs.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What are the payment methods?</summary>

For monthly plans, credit card, for annual plans, credit card in up to 12 installments or single payment by bank slip.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between the plans?</summary>

All plans have the same features; the only difference is the number of submissions for each package and the availability of annual and monthly plans.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can the plans be paid in installments?</summary>

We offer both annual and monthly plans. For the annual option, all plans can be paid in up to 12 interest-free installments on your credit card.

</details>

<details>

<summary>At the end of the trial period, do I lose access to the documents signed on the platform?</summary>

No. At the end of the trial period, you will retain access to the documents signed through the platform. To make new submissions, simply purchase one of the ArqSign plans.

</details>
