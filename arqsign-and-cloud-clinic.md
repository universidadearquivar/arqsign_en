# 🟪 ArqSign and Cloud Clinic

<details>

<summary>I want to integrate my Cloud Clinic account with the ArqSign platform for digital document signing. How should I proceed?</summary>

Through the partnership between Clínica nas Nuvens and ArqSign, the solutions are automatically connectable, just follow these steps:

1. Activate your account on Clínica nas Nuvens.
2. Go to the [ArqSign Platform purchase page](https://arquivar.com.br/arqsign/).
3. Choose your plan and complete the purchase.
4. Log in to the ArqSign solution.
5. In your ArqSign account, go to the **"Integrations"** menu to get the information you need to input into Clínica nas Nuvens.
6. In your Clínica nas Nuvens account, go to **Settings > My Company > Integrations > Digital Signature** and enter the requested information.
7. Done! The solutions are now connected! Now, just send the documents for signing, and when completed, they will be available in both software systems.
8. For details on how to use the tool in Clínica nas Nuvens, check the article on [how digital signature works](https://intercom.help/clinicanasnuvens/pt-BR/articles/8486062-assinatura-digital-como-funciona).

</details>
