<summary>How to configure a document to be signed with a digital certificate (digital signature) or without a digital certificate (electronic signature)?</summary>

On the ArqSign Platform, when configuring a signature flow, you can determine which type of signature should be executed by each recipient by selecting one of the following options:w the steps below:

a) **Electronic Signature** (ArqSign generates advanced electronic signatures with legal validity in accordance with MP 2.200-2 of 08/24/2001 and Law 14.063 of 11/23/2020);

b) **Signature with Personal Digital Certificate ICP-Brasil Type** (ArqSign generates qualified digital signatures in accordance with MP 2.200-2 of 08/24/2001 and Law 14.063 of 11/23/2020);

c) **Signature with Personal Digital Certificate (All Types)** (ArqSign generates both electronic and digital signatures using other certificates).

**To determine the signature type, follow these steps:**

1. After uploading the document and configuring the necessary settings, proceed to configure the recipients.
2. When configuring a recipient, in the "Signature Type" field, select one of the options described above.
3. Done! Now, just configure the other recipients and the signature position on the document, then send.

<summary>How are Electronic Signatures classified?</summary>

According to Law No. 14,063, of September 23, 2020, Article 4, electronic signatures are classified as follows:

I – **Simple electronic signature:** a) Allows the signatory to be identified; b) Attaches or associates data to other data in electronic format related to the signatory;

II – **Advanced electronic signature:** One that uses certificates not issued by ICP-Brasil or other means of proving authorship, document integrity, and acceptance between the parties.

III – **Qualified electronic signature:** One that uses an ICP-Brasil digital certificate.

The three types of signatures mentioned in the legislation define the level of trust of the signature, with the qualified electronic signature having the highest level of trust.

The ArqSign Platform is capable of generating both Advanced and Qualified Signatures, fully complying with all legal requirements.

<summary>What is the difference between a digital and electronic signature?</summary>

Simply put, any signature made through an electronic mechanism that identifies the signatory is considered an electronic signature.

In Brazil, we culturally define a signature made electronically using a digital certificate as a digital signature. When executed electronically without a digital certificate, it is referred to as an electronic signature.

Electronic signatures are most commonly used for agreements between companies, between companies and customers, professionals and patients, etc.

Digital signatures are more commonly used for signatures and transactions involving government agencies.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is a digital signature?</summary>

In Brazil, we say that a Digital Signature is a type of electronic signature made using an ICP-Brasil digital certificate, which is like an identity in the virtual world.

It differs from other electronic signatures because it uses encryption and links the digital certificate to the electronic document being signed.

Digital Certificate technology guarantees the integrity of the document and the indisputable authorship of the signatory, making it the most reliable form of electronic signature.

To make a digital signature, you need to acquire an ICP-Brasil Digital Certificate and a trusted electronic signature platform.

</details>

<summary>What is an electronic signature?</summary>

An electronic signature can be defined as an electronic mechanism that identifies the signatory (the person signing) of a document, whether an individual or a legal entity.

In Brazil, the electronic signature is supported by Provisional Measure 2,200-2 of 2001, the Brazilian Civil Code, and the Normative Instructions of ITI – the National Institute of Information Technology.

It is important to note that, culturally in Brazil, a signature executed electronically with a digital certificate is defined as a digital signature, while one executed electronically without a digital certificate is referred to as an electronic signature.
