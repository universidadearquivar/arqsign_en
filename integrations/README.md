ArqSign has its own Integration API, meaning clients and partners can integrate the functionalities of the ArqSign Platform into their solutions. The Integration API allows communication with other applications/software automatically, without the need for user intervention.

## Introduction to the Documentation

[ArqSign](https://arquivar.com.br/arqsign/) is the electronic and digital signature platform from [Arquivar](https://www.arquivar.com.br/). Signatures made through the ArqSign Platform, whether electronic or digital, meet 100% of legal requirements.
