# 💳 Buy Credits

The “Buy Credits” button will be displayed for all users with an active plan (free or paid account). If the user consumes the entire sending package included in their plan, they must purchase additional credits to continue sending documents.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/03 (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click on the image to enlarge.</p></figcaption></figure>

The user can purchase extra credits for sending documents via email and WhatsApp, as well as credits for sending security codes via SMS.

{% hint style="warning" %}
<mark style="color:orange;">**Extra credits are valid for six months.**</mark>
{% endhint %}

1. By clicking “Buy Credits,” the user will be directed to the checkout screen. They must specify the quantity of credits they wish to purchase for each type (email, WhatsApp, and SMS) and click “Continue.”

{% hint style="warning" %}
<mark style="color:orange;">**If the user’s account includes unlimited email sends, they will only be able to purchase extra credits for sending documents via WhatsApp and security codes via SMS.**</mark>
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (7) (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2.  To complete the purchase, the user must enter the billing and payment information and click “Complete Purchase”.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. After the purchase is completed, if the account is shared with other users, all users with an administrator profile for the account will receive an email notification about the acquisition of the extra credits.
